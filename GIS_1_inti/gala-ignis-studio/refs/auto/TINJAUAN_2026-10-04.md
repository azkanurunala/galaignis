## Usul tolak (7), dengan catatan siap pakai

```bash
python run.py tolak L07 --catatan "the transport ship on the landing pad must match sheet K68 exactly: a rounded bus-sized dropship, glossy white hull with purple stripes, a gold winged crest on the side, two short swept-back wings ending in downward-angled cyan engine pods"
python run.py tolak L12 --catatan "the city seen from the window and balcony must be the futuristic city of Nusantara as in sheet L22: towers with joglo-style tiered rooftops, glowing gold batik light lines, monorail tracks; not an old Mediterranean town"
python run.py tolak L33 --catatan "clean futuristic rooftops of Nusantara matching sheet L22: white and grey modern towers with joglo-style tiered roofs and gold trim, sleek metal water tanks, holographic billboards; no brick, no rust, no wooden water barrels"
python run.py tolak L66 --catatan "the city around the monorail must be Nusantara as in sheet L22: towers with joglo-style tiered rooftops and glowing gold batik light lines, greenery on terraces; the monorail must match the one in L22"
python run.py tolak L23 --catatan "the wide establishing shot must show the entire Atomic Monument: a very tall slender spire, about 300 meters, rising high above the plaza, engraved with glowing gold batik lines"
python run.py tolak L26 --catatan "clean futuristic style matching the academy sheets: polished grey and white pipes with cyan lights; no rust, no grime"
python run.py tolak L36 --catatan "exactly three separate ring catwalks circling the spinning ice crystal, one above the other at three different heights"
```

- L07: kapal di hanggar berbentuk pipih panjang tanpa sayap, berbeda dari K68.
- L12: kota di luar balkon bergaya kota tua Eropa, bukan Nusantara.
- L33: atap bata berkarat dan tangki kayu, bertentangan dengan kota futuristik L22.
- L66: kota di sekitar monorail kota modern biasa, tanpa atap joglo dan garis batik.
- L23: hanya pangkal monumen yang terlihat; ciri utamanya (menara 300 meter) tidak tampak di bidikan lebar.
- L26 (gagal QC model): karat dan kotor, tidak cocok dengan gaya bersih seri. Saya setuju dengan juri.
- L36 (gagal QC model): hanya dua cincin catwalk, bukan tiga. Saya setuju dengan juri.
